The business in plain English

1. Business model

Halozyme licenses ENHANZE, an enzyme that lets drug makers turn intravenous biologics (hours in an infusion chair) into an injection under the skin that takes minutes. Partners pay upfront fees, milestones and a royalty on every dose sold for the life of the patents. Halozyme also sells its own products and auto-injector devices, and bought Elektrofi (high-concentration formulation, Nov 2025) and Surf Bio (Q4 2025) to extend the platform [P1].

Q2 2026 revenue was $481.0M, up 48%; royalties $307.7M, up 50%. 2026 guidance raised to $1.835–1.910B revenue and $8.65–9.00 non-GAAP EPS [P1].

Largest royalty sources: VYVGART Hytrulo (argenx) and DARZALEX SC (Janssen) [P1].

Revenue by type, Q2 2026 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026, press release):

2. Moat

The moat is patents plus a long list of approved partner drugs. Once a partner launches an ENHANZE version and patients switch, the partner pays royalties for years, and regulators have approved ENHANZE in many products, which lowers the risk for the next partner [P1] [P3].

  • Alteogen (Korea) offers a competing enzyme and won Merck's Keytruda SC [P4].
  • Partners can also develop their own subcutaneous formulations.

What Halozyme lacks: control over expiry. Royalty terms on Herceptin SC, MabThera SC, Phesgo and HyQvia run to about 2030, and the VYVGART rate steps down in 2029 [P3].

3. Catalysts, next 12 months

  • Q3 2026 results, 5 Nov 2026 after the close [N1]. Watch royalty revenue against the raised $1.220–1.245B full-year guide [P1].
  • FY2026 results with 2027 guidance, ~Feb 2027.
  • Merck litigation: Dutch court injunction (7 Oct 2026) covering eight European countries, German injunction (Dec 2025), US suit pending [P4]. Undated, but a US win or settlement could add royalties.
  • Negative watch: new partner deals going to Alteogen instead of Halozyme.

4. Asymmetry

Floor: ~11.8× 2027 earnings, ~$1.25B of 2026 adjusted EBITDA guided, and at least $400M of buybacks this year [P1]. Royalties need almost no capital.

Ceiling: a Merck settlement or US court win on Keytruda SC, and Hypercon (from Elektrofi) extending the franchise past 2030.

Stronger side: the floor, for the 1–3 year window. But the mean analyst target (~$109, 9 analysts) is below the 23 Oct close of $125.63 [Y3], short interest is high (~18%), and the market is pricing the post-2029 royalty decline.

5. Bull and bear

Bull: a royalty machine growing 30–40% with expanding margins, a shrinking share count and a forward P/E near 12. New partner drugs keep launching, and the Merck fight could turn into extra royalties.

Bear: the growth peaks before 2030, when several big royalty streams step down or end. Alteogen has shown it can win the biggest drug in the market, and the balance sheet carries ~$2.1B of convertible debt.
